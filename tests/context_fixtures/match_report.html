Patel's late burst seals the final for Northshire

By a test author

Northshire won a tense final against Southvale after a spell of reverse swing from their opening bowler turned a comfortable chase into a collapse in the last hour of the evening session.

Southvale had looked in control while their captain batted with patience through the middle overs, rotating the strike and waiting for the bad ball rather than forcing the pace on a slow surface.

The match turned when Patel returned for a second spell with the older ball. He beat the outside edge three times in an over before trapping the captain in front, and the lower order could not cope with the late movement.

Afterwards the Northshire coach praised the patience of his bowlers and said the plan had always been to keep the ball dry and wait for the surface to break up in the final session.
