Arjun Patel

2 January 2025

Arjun Patel is a right-arm fast-medium bowler known for his control with the older ball and his ability to reverse swing it late in an innings on dry surfaces.

He came through the Northshire academy as a batter who bowled a little, and only took bowling seriously after a coach noticed how much the ball moved for him in the nets during long afternoon sessions.

Team-mates describe him as quiet and methodical, a bowler who studies batters' footwork and plans his spells over by over rather than relying on pace alone.
